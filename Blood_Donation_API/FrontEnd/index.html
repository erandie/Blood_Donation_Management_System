Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about donating or receiving blood? We’re here to clear things up!

How can I become a blood donor on FairyCare?

Just sign up, fill in your blood type and availability, and you're all set to save lives! We’ll notify you when someone nearby needs your help.

Is my personal data safe on this platform?

Absolutely. We use secure authentication, encrypted storage, and follow best practices to keep your info safe and private.

What should I do in case of a blood emergency?

Use our Emergency Alert System to instantly notify nearby donors. They’ll receive real-time messages and location info to respond ASAP.

Can I donate funds instead of blood?

Yes! We support financial donations through secure methods like PayPal to help patients in need of treatment and transport.

How do I know if a donor is available?

Our system automatically finds nearby donors with matching blood types and shows their availability in real-time.

Can I manage my past donations?

Yes, your dashboard shows donation history, upcoming events, and lets you manage your preferences anytime.
