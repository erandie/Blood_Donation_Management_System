Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We’ve got answers — here’s everything you need to know about using FairyCare.

How do I send an emergency alert to donors?

Just tap the "Send Alert" button — we'll detect your location and notify nearby donors instantly via email or SMS.

How are donors matched based on my location?

FairyCare uses your GPS coordinates to find donors within a specific radius (e.g. 10km). Only donors who match your blood type and are nearby will be contacted.

Is it free to send emergency alerts?

Yes! All alerts via email are 100% free. For SMS, charges may apply depending on the provider used (e.g., Twilio).

Can I register as both a donor and a requester?

Absolutely. You can donate blood when someone nearby needs help and request blood during emergencies too — all from your FairyCare profile.

How do I update my location if I move?

Log in to your FairyCare account, go to Profile Settings, and update your current location. This ensures accurate alerts and matches.

Is my personal data safe?

Yes, your data is protected with secure encryption. We only share your contact details with verified patients during real emergencies.
